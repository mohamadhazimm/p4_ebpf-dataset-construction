## Result

The benign probe sent 50,000 uniquely sequence-marked UDP multicast datagrams
and made 50,000 `stat` calls. It used the v10 runner (schema
`2026-10-19.10`, SHA-256
`f5efc2ae05f58250446170cacc534706060ec8210b1feaf5819aae1e95158313`) and the
1,000-ms default tcpdump drain on isolated internal `malnet`.

The PCAP contains all 50,000 unique workload sequence IDs: zero missing and
zero duplicates. It contains 50,026 total frames. Tcpdump reports 50,064
received by filter, 50,026 captured, and zero kernel drops, leaving a counter
discrepancy of 38. The runner therefore marks the run `rejected` with
`packet_capture_counter_discrepancy:38`, as intended. eBPF reported zero lost
events.

This establishes that the sequence-marked packets generated by this benign
probe are present in this PCAP. It does not explain the extra 38 tcpdump
received-by-filter count, prove that all unrelated/background traffic was
captured, or establish that the same pattern holds under malware workloads.
Drain-length repeats were not monotonic: 500 ms produced gaps of 1 or 24 in
separate runs, 1,000 ms produced 0 or 1, and 2,000 ms produced 24. Do not
claim drain time alone fixes the underlying discrepancy.
